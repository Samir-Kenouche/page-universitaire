Researcher Profile

Prof. Samir Kenouche is a Senior Researcher in Theoretical & Quantum Chemistry at the Department of Matter Sciences, Faculty of Exact Sciences, University of Mohamed Khider Biskra, Algeria.

His research sits at the intersection of Quantum Chemistry and Mathematical Chemistry. He develops exact analytical formulations within Quantum Chemical Topology (QTAIM/QCT): pair-density reconstructions, the exchange-correlation hole, electron delocalization response functions and non-local reactivity indices.

Flagship Contribution: the Dual Delocalization Descriptor in Multiwfn v2026.10.1

The Dual Delocalization Descriptor (DDD) is my most important contribution. It is defined through the first-order and second-order responses of the interatomic delocalization index to variations in the total electron number. The DDD gives a bond-resolved measure of electron redistribution upon electron addition or removal: positive (negative) values indicate a net increase (decrease) in interatomic electron sharing, set by the relative weight of the electron-attachment and electron-removal channels.

Δδ(𝒜, ℬ) = ∂δ(𝒜, ℬ)∂N+ − ∂δ(𝒜, ℬ)∂N−
  • Differentiability as a key requirement: bilinear forms (Ángyán–Loos–Mayer type) give well-defined, finite response functions that decouple inter-orbital and self-delocalization contributions. Square-root functionals (Müller type) have no well-defined one-sided derivative with respect to electron number within the PPLB ensemble framework.
  • Neutral reference state only: the analytical DDD recovers bond-resolved finite-difference trends across a series of aromatic systems while relying exclusively on the electronic properties of the neutral species.
  • Available in Multiwfn: implemented by Prof. Tian Lu in the Conceptual DFT module of Multiwfn, the most widely used quantum chemistry post-processing program.
